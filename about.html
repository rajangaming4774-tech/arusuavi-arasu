The founder

Late Dr. N. Nataraja Iyer (1926–2018)

At just eleven, Nataraja Iyer had already spent years assisting his grandfather, Subramania Iyer, in the culinary arts. Nearly nine decades later he would still recall his grandfather’s words: “There is cooking, and then there is cooking with creativity, zeal and affection. The effort you invest in a recipe is just as crucial as the ingredients you select.”

He began as a modest server at the Modern Hotel in Trichy, moved to Madras in 1952 to serve at the MIT Hostel in Chromepet, and became a chef at the renowned Geetha Cafe. In 1956, on a generous proposal from Shri M.N. Sambamoorthy Iyer, Chef Nataraja Iyer became Caterer Nataraja Iyer.

Former President of India, Bharat Ratna Shri V.V. Giri, gave him the title “Arusuvai Arasu”, the Emperor of Six Tastes. He catered at Rashtrapati Bhavan for President R. Venkataraman and served over a million meals at 100,000+ events in a career spanning more than 75 years.

Until he passed at 92, he could be seen in his iconic white khadi shirt, veshti and thundu, vibhuti on his forehead, overseeing the chopping of vegetables and the serving of every meal with a discerning eye. That tradition continues to this day.
