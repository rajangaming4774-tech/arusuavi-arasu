1937

An apprentice at eleven

Young Nataraja Iyer learns the culinary arts at the side of his grandfather, Subramania Iyer, who teaches him that a recipe is lifeless without a soul.

i

1952

Arrival in Madras

From a server at Modern Hotel, Trichy, to the MIT Hostel kitchens in Chromepet, and then chef at the renowned Geetha Cafe.

ii

1956

The chef becomes a caterer

On a generous proposal from Shri M.N. Sambamoorthy Iyer, Chef Nataraja Iyer begins catering for weddings and celebrations.

iii

Honour

“Arusuvai Arasu”

The title, Emperor of Six Tastes, is conferred by former President of India, Bharat Ratna Shri V.V. Giri.

iv

Grandeur

Rashtrapati Bhavan & beyond

Caterer at Rashtrapati Bhavan for President R. Venkataraman, the 1008 Kalyanam and the Mahamaham festival.

v

2020

Feasts that travel

When the pandemic paused celebrations, we brought the wedding feast home with wedding food delivery.

vi

Today

The fourth generation

Led by Chairman Sridhar Natarajan and Director Kamesh Narayanan, trusted by India's most celebrated families, and still cooking with soul.
